# Simple Glossary 0.1.1 — release notes

The first release of Simple Glossary, an Obsidian plugin for desktop. It marks the terms from your glossary note wherever they appear in your other notes, and explains each one in a tooltip when you hover over it.

## What it does

- Reads a glossary note of list items such as `**ADM**: Audio Definition Model`, `[RAG](url) Retrieval-Augmented Generation` or `[[SYMCA]]: South Yorkshire Mayoral Combined Authority`. Hashtags are removed from the explanation, and the first web address becomes a link in the tooltip.
- Marks terms in Reading view and while editing.
- Matches whole words only, and is case-sensitive.
- Matches simple plurals ("BAs"), unless you turn that off.
- By default, marks only the first time each term appears in a note.
- Leaves code, headings, tags, web addresses, frontmatter and comments alone.
- Links to a term, such as `[[SYMCA]]`, get the tooltip as well, and still work as links.
- Clicking a term in Reading view opens the glossary at that entry. While editing, Ctrl-click it (Cmd-click on a Mac). The tooltip also has an "Open in glossary" link.
- When you choose the glossary note in settings, matching notes are suggested as you type. The plugin reloads the glossary whenever you save it, and follows it if you rename or move it.

## Privacy

Simple Glossary reads your notes and never changes them. It makes no network requests.
